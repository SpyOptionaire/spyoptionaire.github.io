Supply (blue) = the next levels above price, demand (pink) = the next levels below; EMAs are the daily EMAs. Away = distance from the last close.

Odds price hits it = how often, over the ticker's last 10 years, price traded at least that far from the close within the next 1 or 5 sessions (measured in daily ranges). History, not a prediction: a level can be hit and still not hold.

IMBALANCED = price closed and is holding under its latest demand (or over its latest supply). Buyers (sellers) first need to close back over (under) it.
