How to read it. Supply = the next levels above price (blue), demand = the next levels below (pink); EMAs are labelled. Away is the distance from the last close in points or dollars. 1 day / 5 days is how often, over the ticker's last 10 years, price traded at least that far from the close (measured in daily ranges, ATR) within the next 1 or 5 sessions. It is history, not a prediction, and a level can be reached and still not hold.

IMBALANCED = price closed and is holding under its latest daily demand (or over its latest daily supply); buyers (sellers) first need to CLOSE back over (under) it.
