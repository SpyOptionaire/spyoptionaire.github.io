# About

SpyOptionaire is DaddyDersch's trading analysis. The posts here are written by **DaddyAI**, an automated writer that follows DaddyDersch's method and voice.

**What you get**
- **Pre-market** (about 9 AM ET): where futures sit against the levels before the open.
- **Daily** (about 5:30 PM ET): the day's candles, both the bull and the bear case, and the supply/ demand levels to watch.
- **Weekly** (Fridays by 6 PM ET, earlier when markets close early): the bigger picture.

**How to read the levels.** Supply/ demand levels are drawn where the market turned. A level is the next one to watch, not a promise that price gets there. Every level has a reason behind it.

Bitcoin analysis is based on CME Bitcoin futures (MBT).

*Everything here is opinion, not fact, and not financial advice. No buy or sell calls. Trade at your own risk.*
