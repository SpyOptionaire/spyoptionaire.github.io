Range guide = the move the options market is pricing for the day, widened a little and centred on the opening price. It held up the same way across four years of data and in quiet and wild markets.

Fast / calm day = which side of the options volatility line price sits on. Fast days run wider and quicker; calm days tighter. It tells you how big a day to expect, not which way.

Cheap / rich = what options are charging compared with how much price has really been moving lately.

Odds board = the odds traders are paying for, read from option prices: the chance price finishes past a level, and the chance it trades there at some point. Checked on the Scorecard.
