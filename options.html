Day box = the move the options market is pricing for the day, widened a little and centred on the opening price. Tested on about 4 years of intraday data (2023-26) and in low and high volatility alike.

Calm / fast zone = which side of the options volatility line price sits on. Under it, days have run wider; over it, calmer. It sizes the day; it does not call direction.

Options cheap / rich = what options are pricing compared with how much price has actually been moving lately.

What we tested and left off because it did not help: put/call ratios, the "pin" strike, open-interest build-ups and big-trade flow.
