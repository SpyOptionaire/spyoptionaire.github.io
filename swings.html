Fear-day snap-backs from November, priced on real option quotes: bought at the ask, sold at the bid at each time the next day, after fees. Hypothetical, not live trades. From the next live swing on, each one gets added here.

How the swing trade works
  • When: only on days when the whole market is fearful and the selling is broad across many names, and only for names stretched unusually far below where they've been trading. Single-stock news drops don't count.
  • The idea: a call bought near the close (3:45 - 3:55 PM) for a one-night bounce. It's not a multi-day hold.
  • The contract: about 1 strike in the money, expiring in about 3 - 7 days rather than the next day. The next-day expiry is the lotto version.
  • Size: a small, fixed slice of the account per swing day.
