Fear-day snap-back: the plan
  • When: the market is fearful (VIX 20+) and the selling is broad: 6 or more of the 42 names stretched far below where they've been trading. ETFs first, up to 5 names. A lone stock dropping on its own news doesn't count.
  • Contract: a call at the money, the nearest expiry 1 - 3 days out.
  • In and out: buy 3:45 - 3:55 PM. Sell on a +30% pop or by noon the next day, whichever comes first.
  • Size: the same small dollar amount every time. Count the whole premium as the risk: the worst trades lost {{ O.fear.worst }}% of it.
