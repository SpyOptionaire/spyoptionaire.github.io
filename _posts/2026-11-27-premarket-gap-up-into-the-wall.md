Bears want to push back under the 8ema at 722.45/ 7267.53, then the overnight low at 7236.50 and a gap fill to Wednesday's close at 716.70/ 7196.75. That would tell me the overnight rip got sold. The bigger bear trigger hasn't changed: a CLOSE under the 689.81/ 6966.75 demand.
