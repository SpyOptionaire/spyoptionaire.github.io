Half day today guys: stocks close at 1:00 PM ET and ES/NQ at 1:15 PM ET, so expect thin volume. The weekly post comes out tonight.

Futures ripped while stocks were closed for Thanksgiving. ES is up about 116 points (+1.6%) and NQ about 614 (+2.4%) from Wednesday's close, with tech leading this time. Both pushed back over the daily 8ema overnight, but both overnight highs stalled right under resistance. That wall is the story for the open.

### SPY/ ES

ES is at 7312.75, which puts SPY around 728. The overnight high at 7343 stopped about 16 points under 7359, old support that flipped to resistance. Right above it sits a stacked wall of old weekly demand, the 20ema and the 200ema, up to the 7404 weekly supply. On SPY that's the 729-735 zone, with both EMAs inside it.

Bulls want the 8ema at 722.45 (ES 7267.53) to hold on any opening dip. The real test is a CLOSE back over the 729-735 zone (ES 7359-7368). With the 200ema sitting right in there too, I wouldn't expect that wall to give way easily on a half day.

Bears want price back under the 8ema, then the overnight low at 7236.50 and a gap fill to Wednesday's close at 716.70 (ES 7196.75). That would tell me the overnight rip got sold. The bigger bear trigger hasn't changed: a CLOSE under the 689.81 demand (ES 6966.75).

Momentum backs the strength a little more here than on ES: NQ's daily momentum has climbed out of the extreme bear zone, while QQQ's is still stuck there from Wednesday's close.

Bulls want the 8ema at 645.48 (NQ 26555) to hold, then a CLOSE through the 26888 supply, which lines up with QQQ's 651-656 zone of old support turned resistance. Clear that and there's room up to the 20ema at 665.47.
